Not a workflow: workflows and building blocks read this when a step names it.

A ticket is its description plus its comments. Read the thread every time, in order, and treat it as part of the request: comments add requirements, narrow scope, or overturn the description, and the newest word wins. Fold them into the plan as work items, and when a comment contradicts the description, say which you followed.
